For GCVE.eu, there are two sets of logos: one set features the generic logos of the GCVE.eu initiative, and the other is for GNAs — GCVE Numbering Authorities.

## GCVE - Global CVE Allocation System

![GCVE.eu logo](https://gcve.eu/gcve-logos/GCVE/PNG/GCVE-color.png)
![GCVE.eu icon](https://gcve.eu/gcve-logos/GCVE/PNG/GCVE-icon.png)

All GCVE logos are available in various formats, including PNG, JPEG, and SVG, at the following location: [https://gcve.eu/gcve-logos/GNA/](https://gcve.eu/gcve-logos/GCVE/)

## GNA - GCVE Numbering Authority

If you have applied and are a registered GNA, you may display one of the following logos on your publications, including public websites, leaflets, or any other content.

![GCVE.eu GNA](https://gcve.eu/gcve-logos/GNA/PNG/GNA-color.png) 
![GCVE.eu GNA icon](https://gcve.eu/gcve-logos/GNA/PNG/GNA-icon.png)

All GNA logos are available in various formats, including PNG, JPEG, and SVG, at the following location: [https://gcve.eu/gcve-logos/GNA/](https://gcve.eu/gcve-logos/GNA/)
