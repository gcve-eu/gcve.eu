We will be at the first FIRST.org hackathon, organized during the [FIRST Annual Conference in Copenhagen](https://www.first.org/conference/2025/).

The hackathon, **FIRSTCON25**, will take place in person at the **37th Annual FIRST Conference** on **Sunday, 22nd June**, in Copenhagen.

During the hackathon, various topics and projects (not limited to the following) can be tackled:

- Improving the software stack to support and distribute the directory.
- Enhancing existing BCPs.
- Reviewing and assisting in the creation of new BCPs.
- Improving the [vulnerability-lookup.org](https://vulnerability-lookup.org/) software stack used by GCVE.eu users.

For more details about the [proposed GCVE activities at the FIRST.org hackathon](https://discourse.ossbase.org/t/gcve-eu-processes-standards-bcp-and-tooling/95), and to follow live updates, visit the discussion link.

If you would like to join the hackathon, you can register at [https://pretix.eu/circl/hackathonfirst25/](https://pretix.eu/circl/hackathonfirst25/).
